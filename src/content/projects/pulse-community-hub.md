## A place to find your people

Pulse needed to make a busy calendar feel approachable. The experience starts with the next useful action: find an event, meet a member, or explore a resource.

The visual system uses generous spacing, direct language, and a flexible card structure that gives each community activity enough room to be understood at a glance.

## Outcome

The new hub gave the collective one dependable place to publish its program and helped members move from browsing to joining with less friction.
